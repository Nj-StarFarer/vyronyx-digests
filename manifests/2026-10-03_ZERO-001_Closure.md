## Lessons (Opus)
1. The baseline is strong: a single threshold on the change in orbit size catches 90% of logged burns with 18 false alarms in 1,233 quiet satellite windows.
2. ZERO's combined score was worse at the far tail than its strongest single ingredient. To reach 90% recall its threshold had to be very low.
3. Its persistence clue pointed the wrong way (weight -1.78): in this data a real burn often looks unsettled across two orbit records, not one.
4. The protocol did its job. The gate caught a weak detector before the sealed answer key was used.

Burn detection is parked until NJ reopens it as a new task with its own frozen specification. A new task could still be tested on the sealed 2024-25 box, because nobody has seen it.
