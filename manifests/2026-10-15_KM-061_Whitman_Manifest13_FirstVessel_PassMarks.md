## The pass marks (one vessel, silent shadow mode, Whitman read-only)
| Measure | GREEN | AMBER | RED |
|---|---|---|---|
| Confirmed abnormalities warned before maintenance found them | ≥ 50% | 30–50% | < 30% |
| Median warning time | ≥ 48 h | 12–48 h | < 12 h |
| False alarms, per vessel | ≤ 1 / month | 1–3 / month | > 3 / month |
| Sure-wrong (wrong cause given with confidence above 80%) | ≤ 5% | 5–15% | > 15% |
| Calibration error (scored only with 20+ named calls) | ≤ 0.10 | 0.10–0.20 | > 0.20 |
| Right subsystem | ≥ 60% | 40–60% | < 40% |
| Evidence completeness | 100% | ≥ 95% | < 95% |

**Rules:**
- **Length:** at least 3 months. Extended up to 12 months until 8 confirmed abnormalities exist; fewer than 8 is reported as "too few events to score", never as a pass.
- **Who judges:** findings are recorded without seeing Whitman's warning, and checked by an adjudicator who is not from VyroNyx.
- **No moving the goalposts:** any change starts a new pilot with new numbers. It never changes a running one.
